# Problem statement and scope

## Problem statement

A football shirt retailer carries club, season, style and size variants. Informal tracking can hide size-specific shortages and make it hard to reconcile receipts with sales. YNWA STORE provides a single inventory view and a record of stock changes.

## Scope

The project covers catalogue management, stock balances, receipts, stock-outs, minimum-stock alerts, reports and CSV export. It is a local Python demonstration using SQLite. It does not process orders or payments, connect to suppliers, or synchronize data across devices.

## Target users

Small football merchandise retailers and shop staff who need to manage jersey stock by club, season, style and size.

## High-level features

- Review inventory totals and restock alerts.
- Search, filter, add and remove jersey variants.
- Record receipts and sales with quantity validation and an audit history.
- Review stock by club and size and export catalogue data.
